## Interpretation and classification

- **ExpDis:** novelty-driven exploration, filtering, distillation, and student
  RL form separate stages. Its mathematical reasoning results do not establish
  general long-horizon agent gains. Compute comparisons must include filtering
  and distillation costs.
- **Internal computation:** PSN-RLVR perturbs parameters and corrects
  sampling-update mismatch; 3PO samples a learned parameter posterior; QeRL
  uses quantization noise; ESRL changes discrete expert paths and replays them
  in optimization. Their common perturbation tag does not make these
  mechanisms interchangeable. 3PO's approximately matched FLOPs do not mean
  matched wall-clock time: the reported M3PO/C3PO implementation takes about
  1.5 times as long as GRPO.
- **Strategy exploration:** PSRA allocates a fixed budget among problem–strategy
  arms. Its validation covers Qwen2.5 up to 7B. A uniformly unsuccessful rollout
  group is not proof of missing model capability.
- **Memory construction:** DAEDALUS and RSIAgent create reusable memory through
  environment practice and freeze it for evaluation. They belong to
  environment-space learning because interaction creates reusable experience;
  this classification does not imply parameter updates or continual online
  memory learning. DAEDALUS's LLM judge and self-written success conditions are
  imperfect substitutes for official verifiers. RSIAgent's curriculum uses
  target-query references and must not be described as entirely task-agnostic.
- **Memory and action:** MemAct studies historical retrieval, acquisition of new
  views, and stopping decisions in spatial QA. Prompt-Driven Exploration uses
  language conditions to change rollout behavior; its September revision is
  distinguished from its July first submission.
- **Metrics:** RPD's step-level divergence, approach-level mathematical diversity,
  and Inquiry Learning's experimental-action annotations measure different
  objects. A useful proxy or downstream improvement does not establish a
  universal exploration metric. The approach-diversity paper explicitly
  demonstrates judge-preference exploitation under direct reward optimization.
